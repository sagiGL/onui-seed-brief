UI for systems that can't move to the cloud

Any screen, on the backend you already have your data never leaves.

onUI installs as one SDK and turns your existing product into building blocks. It reads what each user needs and assembles the right screen for them live, no code, nothing migrated. Built for the teams cloud tools lock out: security, health, finance.

The Problem
Shipping UI is the bottleneck. Every new screen, report, or per-customer view waits weeks in an engineering queue.
The modern tools that make this instant cloud CMSs and AI app builders only work if your data and backend live in their cloud. That rules out the teams moving the most sensitive data: security, health, finance, and serious B2B products. The companies that most need to move fast are the ones still building every screen by hand.
The Solution
Install once. onUI maps your product into blocks, reads each user, and builds the screen they need.
onUI is one SDK you add to your own app. It reads your existing product every screen, API, and data model and turns it into reusable building blocks. From then on anyone can ask, in plain language, for a new dashboard, report, or per-customer view, and onUI builds it from those blocks and places it live in the product editing existing pages or creating new ones, with no extra code and no deploy. Because the agent runs inside your product and is fit to your system, what it builds actually works with your real data and permissions.

Why Now
Users now expect software to behave like the AI tools they use every day generate what I need, adapt to me, instantly.
After ChatGPT and a wave of build-an-app-in-a-prompt tools, static one-size-fits-all screens feel broken. Buyers feel it from both sides: their users demand tailored, on-demand interfaces, and their own teams expect to ship them in minutes, not sprints. That expectation is now universal but the products holding the most valuable data are the ones that can't reach for a cloud tool to meet it. onUI brings the generate-on-demand experience to exactly those products, and every screen it builds teaches it what each role needs next.
